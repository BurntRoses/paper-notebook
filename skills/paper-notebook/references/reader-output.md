# Reader output

The product is an offline reader for AI-generated structured learning content. Generate useful concepts, conditions, reasoning, examples, diagrams, formulas, exercises and sources with the shared renderer. Do not generate conversational product introductions, decorative slogans, duplicate labels, movable text boxes or a custom app for each subject.

## Pages and reading controls

Page outlines and header, right-margin and bottom-margin separators use regular solid lines with consistent weight and color. Keep hand-drawn treatments within lesson figures, annotations and content callouts; do not deform page boundaries or reading controls. Ordinary paragraphs have no decorative side brackets.

Landscape displays one landscape sheet; portrait displays one portrait sheet. The header can switch portrait/landscape paper independently of the screen and enable two pages. Two pages sit side by side on a landscape screen and stack vertically on a portrait screen. Explicit portrait/landscape choices preserve paper proportions. Continuous mode is also available. Default ruling is horizontal lines. Content that fits a fresh sheet moves intact. Only very long prose or lists/tables split; formulas, worked examples, programs and figures stay together. Only the first sheet of each source section carries its large title; continuation sheets use a small running header and return the space to the body. Continuations preserve source IDs and UTF-16 offsets. Oversized technical objects remain accessible in scrolling viewports.

`Page.layout`: reading, cornell, grid or exam. Cornell is the default and uses optional `cues: [{id,text,blockId?,anchor?}]` and `summary`. The optional anchor is an exact phrase in the referenced block: cues follow its physical fragment and line, including formula parameters on continuation sheets. Each question must be answerable from its adjacent passage or figure. Clicking a cue locates its source. Notes can push later entries down without overlap. Summaries state useful recall points. Legacy free layouts remain readable but have no drag or editing controls.

`extensions.readerProfile` supports layout auto/single/spread/scroll; shape auto/portrait/landscape; direction horizontal/vertical; transition turn/cover/slide/none; theme paper-light/paper-night; motion full/reduced; font hand/serif/sans; lineSpacing 1.65/1.85/2.05/2.25 (default 2.05); marginNotes and bottomNotes true/false. Night mode changes paper, UI, tables and diagrams without dimming a white sheet. Default auto means one sheet matching viewport orientation. OS reduced-motion takes precedence. The reader uses preset transitions, with no freely deformable cloth physics.

Controls stay in the header and footer. Source text is read-only. Selected-text actions support highlight, underline, circle, wave, box, bracket, check, star and emphasis dots. Authored study marks are deterministic; clicking a mark opens a small inspector, and the same action on the same range is merged or toggled instead of stacking duplicates. The paper has a right margin and a bottom margin, each independently switchable. Cornell recall cues appear in the right margin beside their first relevant fragment. Section summaries appear only once, on their final sheet; duplicate summary strings are omitted. Double-click blank margin space to add a simple note; notes and authored cues flow together without overlap. Transparent is the first color dot. Long notes collapse with an expand control. Linked notes follow their source; free notes belong to their sheet. Questions and bookmarks are collected in the top-right button, created from selected passages. The compact selection ribbon stays available until an outside click or Escape. Images, diagrams and charts can be enlarged to draw circles, pen strokes, checks, arrows and short text labels. Personal image marks remain in userState, separate from authored figure.annotations. A short beginner guide and an interactive tutorial are included in every downloaded HTML and can be reopened in settings. Avoid repeated toolbars, conversational introductions and product descriptions on lesson pages.

Require actual bold concepts (`kind: "bold"`) and colored text (`kind: "red", color: "#285f9e"` or `"#b1483d"`) in each teaching section, in addition to native circles and underlines. A colored stroke is not colored text. Review `studyMarks.typography` for missing bold/color pages and inspect the live text. Include sparse authored `study.marks` in the default lesson source; available annotation tools alone do not produce premarked content. Each target must be an exact phrase in the actual block text. Builders and verifiers report mark counts/kinds and reject nonempty text tutorials with no marks, unless the user explicitly requested unmarked content and `--allow-unmarked` is used. Empty readers are exempt. Authored marks remain in both personal and public HTML copies; personal annotations remain a separate export choice.

Use `study.visual` for a small number of subject-appropriate treatments: `blackboard` for a formula or decisive principle, `extract` for a quotation or definition, and `sketch` for a relationship diagram. Keep the text live and the treatment subordinate to the explanation. Pages may set `subject` to `stem`, `literature`, `business`, `code` or `general` to tune diagrams, quotations, code containers and emphasis.

Use at most three `study.explanations: [{text,note}]` when a key concept or code expression needs a line-linked explanation. Each text must occur in the actual block. Notes sit beside the relevant line or below it on narrow pages; keep explanations brief enough to read with their source. Code syntax coloring preserves exact text, indentation and selector offsets. Stars and check marks sit at the upper-right text edge in the interline space; avoid covering letter centers.

Before generation, use the brief interview to capture goal, prior knowledge, language style, time horizon, route, material fidelity, references and terminology. For a PDF, document, image or scan, OCR page by page, retain the raw recognition with page locators, list uncertain characters and corrections separately, then build a controlled glossary. In `strict` material fidelity, preserve supplied questions, answers, order and objective claims; do not silently repair or supplement them. The brief is source metadata, not a replacement for evidence.

## Saving and delivery

User records remain separate from lesson content. IndexedDB restores local drafts. Downloads include all personal records by default; users may choose a public copy. Preserve legacy original stroke samples and visibly mark unresolved coordinates or text anchors instead of guessing.

Generated HTML must open from file:// with networking disabled. Fonts, runtime, media and formulas stay embedded. Deliver one title-and-revision named offline HTML by default. It embeds source and personal records; ZIP/JSON are optional advanced exports. Reading, marking, answering and saving records take priority over arbitrary future editing. The PDF panel repaginates onto light A4 portrait/landscape sheets (12pt, at least 12mm margins), optionally includes records, and uses system print. Anchored marks reflow; freehand stays in original-page appendices. Embedded assets are not copied a second time into the bundle. The default reader contains common-character hand fonts, including GB2312 and the UI core; uncommon characters remain readable through system fonts. The builder removes unused font slices in authored books. Do not add full Unicode fonts, unused high-resolution images or promotional media. Download only the lesson's necessary assets and embed them before delivery. Validate, build, inspect desktop/tablet/phone layouts, exercise an actual quiz, export a personal copy, reopen offline and export again. State physical-device checks that were not performed.

Use native `chart` blocks for accurate scatter, line and bar graphics. `study.role` separates definitions, intuition, derivations, worked examples, evidence and takeaways; `study.frame` selects stable auto/plain/pencil/tape/double/margin treatments. Comparison tables accept `align` per column; headers center and numeric columns center by default. `keepTogether` preserves meaningful units. See components for exact fields.
